The tenant's members and invitations

- [`member list`](#almena-member-list) — List members and pending invitations
- [`member invite`](#almena-member-invite) — Invite someone by email (admins); they join when they next sign in with it
- [`member uninvite`](#almena-member-uninvite) — Take an invitation back before it is accepted (admins)
- [`member update`](#almena-member-update) — Change a member's role (admins); a tenant keeps one admin at least
- [`member remove`](#almena-member-remove) — Remove a member (admins); whoever signs for the tenant stays until replaced
- [`member leave`](#almena-member-leave) — Leave the tenant yourself

## `almena member list`

List members and pending invitations

```text
Usage: almena member list [OPTIONS]
```

## `almena member invite`

Invite someone by email (admins); they join when they next sign in with it

```text
Usage: almena member invite [OPTIONS] --email <EMAIL>
```

```text
Options:
      --email <EMAIL>
          Their email

      --role <ROLE>
          Their role in the tenant
          
          [default: member]
          [possible values: admin, member]
```

Usage: almena member uninvite [OPTIONS] <ID>
