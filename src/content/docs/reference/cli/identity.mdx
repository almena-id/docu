The tenant's identities (its register of DIDs)

- [`identity list`](#almena-identity-list) — List the tenant's identities, newest first
- [`identity get`](#almena-identity-get) — Show one: its DID, who uses it, and the document it publishes
- [`identity create`](#almena-identity-create) — Register an identity (pending until its first log entry is signed)
- [`identity update`](#almena-identity-update) — Rename it (its DID stays; whatever acts as it keeps its own name)
- [`identity delete`](#almena-identity-delete) — Delete it (admins): only one nothing acts as and nobody signed
- [`identity sign`](#almena-identity-sign) — Sign its next did:webvh log entry from your wallet

## `almena identity list`

List the tenant's identities, newest first

```text
Usage: almena identity list [OPTIONS]
```

```text
Options:
      --limit <LIMIT>
          Items per page (1–100)
          
          [default: 20]

      --cursor <CURSOR>
          Start after this cursor (a page's `next_cursor`)

      --all
          Follow `next_cursor` until the last page
```
