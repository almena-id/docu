## `almena tenant update`

Change the tenant's name, mediator, signing flow or languages (admins)

```text
Usage: almena tenant update [OPTIONS]
```

```text
Options:
      --name <NAME>
          Its new name

      --mediator <MEDIATOR>
          The mediator its identity receives messages through (see `almena mediator choices`)

      --no-mediator
          Remove its mediator

      --signing-flow <SIGNING_FLOW>
          Who signs as the tenant

          Possible values:
          - any-admin:   Any of its admins
          - single-user: One member, named with --signer

      --signer <SIGNER>
          The member who signs, under `single-user` (their user id; see `almena member list`)

      --language <LANGUAGES>
          The languages it works in, of the platform's: all of them, repeated or comma-separated
          (`--language en,es`); the trust anchor's are all
```
