Something does not work — the catalog does not open, a code does not
answer? Before anything else, open
[status.almena.id](https://status.almena.id). It says whether each part of
Almena works right now, and how each has done over the last 90 days. It is in
English only, and always dark.

It runs on its own, away from the rest of Almena, so it stays up and tells
you when the rest is down.

- The band <Mark n={1} /> is the whole network in one line, and when it was
  last updated.
- The filters <Mark n={2} /> show only one kind of service: **Identity**,
  **Mediators**, **APIs**, **Agents**, **Portals**, or **Third-party
  services** — what Almena relies on from others, such as its sign-in
  providers. **All** shows everything.
- Each service <Mark n={3} />: its name, who runs it, its address, its state
  now, and a bar per day for the last 90 days with the share of time it
  worked. Point at a day (or tap it) for its figures.

## What the states mean

For the whole network, in the band:

| The band says | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **All systems operational** | Everything answers as it should. |
| **Degraded performance** | Something answers, but slowly. It works; it may take longer. |
| **Partial outage** | Some services do not answer. What you need may still work. |
| **Major outage** | Nothing answers. Try again later. |
| **Maintenance in progress** | Planned work, announced beforehand. |
| **Status not known yet** | The page has only just started checking. |

For each service:

| The service says | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Operational** | It answers, in good time. |
| **Degraded** | It answers, but takes more than two seconds, or says it is not at its best. |
| **Partial outage** | An incident says part of it does not work. |
| **Outage** | It failed twice in a row, or an incident says it is down. |
| **Maintenance** | Planned work on it, now. |
| **No data** | No recent check: the page cannot say. |

Each day's bar is green when the service always answered, yellow when it was
slow, orange for a short outage (less than 5% of the day), red for a longer
one, blue for planned maintenance and grey when there is no data.

## Incidents and maintenance

When something goes wrong, or work is planned, the people who run Almena
write it on the page: **Ongoing** at the top while it lasts, **Scheduled
maintenance** before it starts, **Past incidents** below. Each one says which
services it affects and how it went, step by step: **Investigating**,
**Identified**, **Monitoring**, **Resolved** — or **Scheduled**, **In
progress**, **Completed** for maintenance. **Incident history** lists every
one of the last 90 days.

## Follow it

You do not need to keep the page open:

- **Atom** (at the foot of the page, or
  [status.almena.id/feed.xml](https://status.almena.id/feed.xml)): a feed of
  the incidents, the maintenance and every outage the checks saw, over the
  last 90 days. Add it to any feed reader, or to a chat channel that reads
  feeds, and you are told as it happens.
- **JSON** (`/api/status.json`): the same as the page, for a program.

<Aside type="tip" title="Everything green, and still not working?">
The page checks Almena from outside. If it says everything works and you
still cannot get through, the problem may be on your side: your connection,
your browser, or your wallet's [mediator](/use/mediator/) if it is not
Almena's.
</Aside>
