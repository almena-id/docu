A **form** is the list of what you ask a person for. The same form works in
two places:

- **To give a credential**: what someone fills in when they apply for it in
  the catalog.
- **To check a credential**: what a [verifier](/use/organisation/verifiers/)
  asks a wallet to show.

A form has **fields** — things the person types or picks, like a name or a
date — and **credentials to present** — credentials they already have in
their wallet and show instead of typing.

## Your forms

Open **Forms** in the side menu, under **Templates**.

<Screenshot name="registry-forms" alt="The list of forms, each with how many fields and credentials it asks for." />

Press **Create** <Mark n={1} /> to make a new one. Each line <Mark n={2} /> is
a form; press it to see its fields and credentials, and open it to edit it.

## Make or change one

**Create** opens an empty form; **Edit**, on an open form, the same screen
filled in.

<Screenshot name="registry-form-edit" alt="A form being edited: its name and description, its fields and the credential it asks to be shown." />

<Steps>

1. Its **Name** <Mark n={1} /> and, if you want, a **Description**
   <Mark n={2} />: what it is for and what people need at hand. The globe in
   each box lets you write it in each of your languages.

2. **Fields** <Mark n={3} />: press **Add field** and search the catalogue
   (given name, date of birth, email…). For each one, tick **Required** or
   not, add a line of **Help** if it helps, and, under **Restrict**, narrow
   what is accepted — a shorter text, a range of dates, only some values.
   The arrows change the order.

3. **Credentials to present** <Mark n={4} />: press **Add credential** and
   choose the kind of credential, for example a membership card. Write the
   **Purpose** the wallet will show ("To check you belong to Nova
   University"), tick the **Claims** — the pieces of it you want to see; only
   those are shared — and choose **Trusted from**: any issuer published in
   Almena, or only the ones you pick.

4. Press **Create** (or **Save changes**) at the bottom.

</Steps>

A credential fills the form's fields with the same name: if the form asks for
a given name and a membership card that has one, the name comes from the
card, already checked.

<Aside type="caution" title="Forms in use">
Once an issuer offers a form or people have answered it, it is **in use**:
you can add optional fields and credentials and change its words, but not
take anything away or make it stricter, and it cannot be deleted.
</Aside>

## DCQL and JSON Schema

An open form has three tabs. You do not need them to use Almena; they are for
whoever connects other systems.

<Screenshot name="registry-form-dcql" alt="The DCQL tab of a form: the query a wallet is asked." />

- **Verify**: paste what a wallet answered, to check it by hand. The
  [Verify tab of a verifier](/use/organisation/verifiers/) does this for you
  with a QR code.
- **DCQL** <Mark n={1} />: the same form, written as the question a wallet
  understands <Mark n={2} />. DCQL is simply the standard way to say "show me
  these credentials, and only these pieces of them".
- **JSON Schema**: the rules the answers must follow — which fields, which
  are required, which formats — written in the standard way programs read.
  Systems use it to check answers before sending them.
