Under **Templates**, in the side menu, beside [Forms](/use/organisation/forms/),
are the pieces forms and credentials are built from. Almena keeps one
catalogue of them for everybody, so that a "date of birth" means the same in
every organisation and every wallet.

| Screen | What it holds | Who changes it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Fields** | The questions a form can ask | Almena's: read only. Your own: with a subscription |
| **Credential types** | The kinds of credential issuers give | Almena's: read only. Your own: with a subscription |
| **Value lists** | Lists of codes some fields pick from | The trust anchor only |
| **Field categories**, **Credential categories** | What the catalogue is sorted under | The trust anchor only |

The **trust anchor** is Almena's own account, which keeps the shared
catalogue. If you are not it, you only see the first two screens.

## Fields

<Screenshot name="registry-fields" alt="The Fields screen: your own fields first, then Almena's, with a search box." />

- <Mark n={1} /> **Your fields**: fields of your own, for something Almena's
  catalogue lacks. Only your organisation sees and uses them. Making them
  comes with a [subscription](/use/organisation/billing/); without one the
  box says so.
- <Mark n={2} /> **Almena's fields**: the same for every organisation, named
  after recognised standards. Press one to see its details: its type, its
  allowed values, its limits.
- <Mark n={3} /> Type here to find one by name, key or tag.

## Credential types

<Screenshot name="registry-credential-types" alt="The Credential types screen: your own types first, then Almena's drawn as cards." />

- <Mark n={1} /> **Your credential types**: credentials of your own, which
  your issuers give and your forms ask for. They also come with a
  subscription.
- <Mark n={2} /> **Almena's credential types**, drawn as cards: identity,
  contact, education, work, membership… Open one to see what it says (its
  claims) and how it is named in each standard format.

An issuer gives the types ticked in its **Credentials** tab
([Set up an issuer](/use/organisation/issuers/)).

## Value lists (trust anchor only)

<Screenshot name="registry-value-lists" alt="The Value lists screen of the trust anchor: countries, languages, sexes, document types, education levels, file formats." />

The lists of codes that fields with a fixed set of answers pick from:
countries, languages, file formats… **Create value list** <Mark n={1} /> adds
one; press a list <Mark n={2} /> to change its codes and their names in every
language. While a field uses a list, codes can be added but never removed.

## Categories (trust anchor only)

<Screenshot name="registry-categories" alt="The Field categories screen: Person, Contact, Documents, Education and work, Membership, Files." />

What fields (**Field categories**) and credential types (**Credential
categories**) are sorted under, each with its name in every language and an
icon. **Create category** <Mark n={1} /> adds one; press one <Mark n={2} /> to
rename it or change its icon. One with something filed under it cannot be
deleted.

<Aside type="note">
Whatever the trust anchor changes here, every organisation sees: it is the
shared catalogue. What you make of your own, only you see.
</Aside>
