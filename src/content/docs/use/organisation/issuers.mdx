An **issuer** is the part of your organisation that gives credentials: a
university's academic office, a club's secretary. Its name is what people
read on the credentials it gives.

## Create it

Open **Issuers** in the side menu.

<Screenshot name="registry-issuers" alt="The list of issuers, with the Create button." />

Your issuers are listed <Mark n={2} />, each tagged with whether it is
published and whether its identity is signed. Press **Create** <Mark n={1} />.

<Screenshot name="registry-issuer-new" alt="The form to create an issuer: name, description and mediator." />

<Steps>

1. Its **name** <Mark n={1} />, as people will read it.

2. A **description** <Mark n={2} />: one line on what it gives. The globe in
   the box lets you write it in each of your languages.

3. The **mediator** <Mark n={3} />: leave the one offered unless you were told
   otherwise.

4. Press **Create** <Mark n={4} />.

</Steps>

## Offer credentials

Open the issuer you created and its **Credentials** tab. Tick the credentials
it gives and, for each, the **form** people fill in to ask for it (make one
first under **Forms** if you have none).

## Sign and publish

Before it appears in the catalog, two things:

<Steps>

1. **Sign its identity.** Press **Sign** on the issuer: a code appears, and
   whoever signs for your organisation scans it with their wallet. This is
   what lets anyone check the credentials it gives.

2. **Publish it.** An administrator turns on **Publish**. From then on it is
   in the catalog, and people can apply.

</Steps>

<Aside type="tip">
Not sure what is left? **Awaiting you**, on the overview, lists it in order,
and says what has to be done first.
</Aside>
