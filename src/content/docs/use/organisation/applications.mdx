Each line is one application <Mark n={1} />: the credential asked for, who
sent it, when and how it stands. Open one.

## Decide

<Screenshot name="registry-application" alt="An application: the credential asked for, the holder's signature, the answers and the decision." />

<Steps>

1. At the top, the credential asked for, as it will look.

2. Check the green line: **signed by the holder**. It means the answers are
   exactly what the person's wallet sent.

3. Read the **answers**.

4. If you want, leave a **note** for the person <Mark n={1} />: they will see
   it with your decision.

5. Press **Reject** <Mark n={2} /> or **Accept** <Mark n={3} />.

</Steps>

## Give the credential

Once accepted, the same page shows what the credential will say, filled in
from the answers. Check it, correct anything, choose until when it holds, and
press **Continue to sign**: a code appears for the issuer's signer — the
person named in the issuer's **Signing** tab — to scan with their wallet.
Once signed, the person who applied takes it from the catalog into their
wallet.

## Suspend or revoke

On the page of a credential you gave, whoever signs for the issuer can
**Suspend** it (for a while), **Reinstate** it, or **Revoke** it (for good).
Each change is signed from the wallet, like the credential itself.
