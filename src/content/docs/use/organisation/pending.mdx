The overview tells you, at a glance, what is waiting for you, what your
issuers and verifiers did and what your organisation still needs to set up.

## Pending

**Pending**, in the side menu under **Activity**, lists everything that is
waiting to be signed, published or given, in the order it has to be done.
The overview shows the first five under **Awaiting you**.

<Screenshot name="registry-pending" alt="The Pending list: an identity to sign, a verifier still a draft and credentials to issue." />

- <Mark n={1} /> Each line is one thing: its name (press it to open it), what
  it is, and why it waits — **Pending signature**, **Draft**, **To issue**…
- <Mark n={2} /> When you are the one who does it, the button to do it:
  **Sign**, **Publish** or **Issue**.
- <Mark n={3} /> When something has to happen first, the line says what —
  for example **Its issuer has no signer.** — and takes you there.

<Aside type="tip">
Signing is done from your Almena wallet. If the list says **Link your Almena
wallet to sign.**, link it first from your
[profile](/use/organisation/tokens/).
</Aside>

## Activity and health

Further down the overview:

<Screenshot name="registry-overview-more" alt="The overview's activity chart, its tiles, and the account health with what is left to set up." />

- <Mark n={1} /> **Activity**: applications sent, credentials issued and
  credentials checked, day by day. Choose **7 days**, **30 days** or
  **90 days**. A red dot above a day means something was rejected or failed.
- <Mark n={2} /> The tiles under the chart: **Applications waiting** for a
  decision, those **Accepted, to issue**, the credentials you issued (valid,
  suspended, revoked) and the checks your verifiers made. Press a tile to go
  to the list behind it.
- <Mark n={3} /> **Account health**: what your organisation needs before it
  works fully — a name, a mediator, someone who signs — with a tick when done
  and the percentage done.
- <Mark n={4} /> Next to anything still to do, **Set up** takes you where it
  is done, and a line says why it matters.
