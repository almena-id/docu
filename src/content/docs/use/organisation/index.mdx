The [registry](https://registry.almena.id) is where your organisation gives
credentials. There you set up who gives them, what people fill in to ask for
them, and you review what arrives.

## Your home screen

When you sign in, the registry opens on the **Overview**: what waits for you
and how your organisation is doing.

<Screenshot name="registry-overview" alt="The registry's overview: the side menu, the account selector, what awaits you and the activity chart." />

- <Mark n={1} /> **The side menu** takes you everywhere: applications, issuers,
  forms, settings.
- <Mark n={2} /> **The account** you are working in. If you belong to more
  than one organisation, change it here.
- <Mark n={3} /> **Awaiting you**: what is waiting to be signed, published or
  given, in the order to do it. Each line takes you where it is done.
- <Mark n={4} /> **Activity**: how many applications arrived, how many
  credentials you gave and how many were checked, day by day.

Further down, **Account health** lists what your organisation still needs
to set up, with a link to each: [What waits for you](/use/organisation/pending/)
explains it all.
