Issuers, verifiers and mediators start as a **Draft**: only your organisation
sees them. **Publishing** one makes it public: its identity (its DID) can be
looked up by anyone, issuers appear in the catalog, and verifiers may ask
wallets for credentials.

<Steps>

1. **Sign first.** Your organisation's identity and the item's own must be
   signed, from the wallet of whoever signs for your organisation. Until
   then, publishing says **Sign its identity first.** or **Sign the account's
   identity first.** [Pending](/use/organisation/pending/) lists what to sign,
   in order.

2. **Publish.** An administrator presses the eye button beside the name
   <Mark n={2} /> — **Publish**. Nothing to scan: it is a switch.

</Steps>

The same button, **Unpublish**, takes it back: the item stays, but leaves the
catalog and stops asking wallets. Its **Status** on the **Summary** tab says
**Published** or **Draft**, and the lists tag each one.

<Aside type="caution">
**Delete** <Mark n={3} /> is not the same as unpublishing: its identity stops
working for good, and it cannot be undone.
</Aside>
