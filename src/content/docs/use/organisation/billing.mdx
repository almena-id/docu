Using Almena — issuers, verifiers, forms, applications, credentials — is
free. A **subscription** adds what goes beyond Almena's shared catalogue.
Open **Billing**, in the side menu under **Account**.

<Screenshot name="registry-billing" alt="The Billing screen: the plan, Free use, and what a subscription adds, each not included." />

- <Mark n={1} /> **Plan**: **Free use**, or your subscription — its plan,
  whether it is **Active**, and until when it is paid.
- <Mark n={2} /> **What a subscription adds**, each **Included** or **Not
  included**:
  - **Fields of your own**, for your forms, when Almena's catalogue lacks one.
  - **Credential types of your own**, which your issuers give and your forms
    ask for.

<Aside type="note">
Payments are not open yet: to subscribe, contact Almena. If a subscription
ends, what you made with it keeps working; you only cannot make or change
more until it is renewed.
</Aside>

See [Templates](/use/organisation/templates/) for where fields and credential
types of your own are made.
