Open [registry.almena.id](https://registry.almena.id). There are no
passwords: you get in with your wallet, or with a code sent to your email.

## With your wallet (recommended)

<Steps>

1. Press **Continue with Almena** <Mark n={1} />.

2. A code appears. Scan it with your Almena wallet: it shows where you are
   signing in <Mark n={3} />. Check that it says **registry.almena.id**, and
   press **Accept** <Mark n={4} />.

   <Screenshot phone from={3} name="wallet-sign-in" alt="The wallet asks to sign in to Almena Registry, at registry.almena.id." />

</Steps>

The first time, this also creates your account. Signing in with the wallet
matters: whatever your organisation signs, it signs from the wallet of the
person in charge.

## With your email

<Steps>

1. Press **Other ways in** <Mark n={2} />.

   <Screenshot name="registry-login" alt="The sign-in page: an email box and the button that sends the code." />

2. Type your email <Mark n={1} /> and press **Send code** <Mark n={2} />.

3. Type the six-digit code that arrives in your inbox.

</Steps>

On the same page you can also continue with Google, Microsoft, Apple or
GitHub.

<Aside type="tip">
You can link more ways in later — your wallet, another email, another
provider — from your **Profile**, in the menu under your initial at the top
right.
</Aside>
