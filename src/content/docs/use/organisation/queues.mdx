If your organisation has its own system — a student records application, a
door controller — it can be told, as it happens, what arrives for an issuer
or a verifier: an application sent, a decision, a credential given or
withdrawn, a credential checked. Each issuer and each verifier can have a
**queue** for that: a mailbox your system reads.

Open the issuer or verifier and its **Queue** tab <Mark n={1} />.

<Screenshot name="registry-queue" alt="An issuer's Queue tab: the queue's name, its user, address and virtual host, and the button to delete it." />

<Steps>

1. An administrator presses **Make the queue**.

2. A password appears, **shown this once**. Copy it into your system's
   settings with the other details <Mark n={2} />: the **Queue**, the
   **User**, the **Address** and the **Virtual host**. The registry does not
   keep the password.

3. Your system connects with them and reads what arrives.

</Steps>

**Delete the queue** <Mark n={3} /> removes it, with any message still in it,
and its user: your system stops reading. You are asked first.

<Aside type="tip">
Whoever connects the system will find the messages and how to read them in
[Queues, in Integrate](/integrate/queues/). A lost password is replaced with the API or the CLI,
not from this screen.
</Aside>
