Several people can work in the same account. Each is an **Admin** or a
**Member**:

- **Member**: works with issuers, verifiers, forms and applications.
- **Admin**: the same, and also adds and manages users, publishes, and
  changes the account's settings.

## The list

Open **Users**, in the side menu under **Account**.

<Screenshot name="registry-users" alt="The users of the account: an admin with a wallet linked and an invited member." />

Everyone in the account is listed, with their role and whether they have a
wallet linked. **Add user** <Mark n={1} /> invites someone. Someone invited who has not come in yet shows as **Invited**
<Mark n={2} />.

The menu at the end of each line <Mark n={3} /> lets an administrator **Make
admin** or **Make member**, **Remove from the account** or, for an
invitation, **Cancel the invitation**. On your own line it offers **Leave the
account**. Each is asked once more before it is done.

## Invite someone

<Screenshot name="registry-user-new" alt="The screen to add a user: email and role." />

<Steps>

1. Press **Add user** on the list.

2. Type their **Email** <Mark n={1} /> and choose their **Role**
   <Mark n={2} />.

3. Press **Send invitation** <Mark n={3} />.

</Steps>

They get an email. They join the next time they sign in to the registry with
that address — no other step.

<Aside type="note">
The last administrator cannot be removed, nor can whoever signs for the
account: choose someone else to sign first, under
[Signing](/use/organisation/settings/).
</Aside>
