In the registry your organisation is an **account**. Its details are under
**Settings**, in the side menu under **Account**. Only administrators change
them; members can read them.

## Name, mediator and languages

<Screenshot name="registry-tenant" alt="The account's settings: name, mediator, the languages ticked and the Save button." />

<Steps>

1. **Name** <Mark n={1} />: your organisation as people will read it.

2. **Mediator** <Mark n={2} />: the service through which wallets reach your
   organisation. Leave Almena's unless you were told otherwise; you can pick
   one of your own or another public one.

3. **Languages** <Mark n={3} />: the languages your organisation works in, at
   least one. Your forms, fields and descriptions are written in these; the
   globe in each text box opens one line per language.

4. Press **Save** <Mark n={4} />.

</Steps>

The **JSON** tab shows your organisation's identity document, for whoever
connects systems.

## Who signs

Some things your organisation does are signed from a wallet: its identity,
its issuers' and verifiers' identities, the credentials it gives. **Signing**,
in the side menu under **Trust**, says who does it.

<Screenshot name="registry-signing" alt="The Signing screen: the signing flow chosen on the left, how it works on the right." />

<Steps>

1. Choose the **Signing flow** <Mark n={1} />:
   - **Any one admin**: any administrator with a linked wallet signs.
     Nobody holds things up, but any admin publishes without another seeing it.
   - **One specific user**: only the member you choose signs. One person
     answers for what is published, but if they are away nothing is.

2. **How it works** <Mark n={2} /> draws the flow you chose, with what it
   gains and what it costs.

3. Press **Save** <Mark n={3} />.

</Steps>

<Aside type="caution">
Whoever signs needs an Almena wallet linked to their profile. Until they
link one, they cannot sign.
</Aside>

Each issuer and verifier also has its own **Signing** tab: the member who
signs its credentials and status changes.
