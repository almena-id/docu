Linking your organisation's domain — `nova.example` — lets others see that
the account is really yours, and lets you run a mediator under it. You prove
it by adding one line, a **TXT record**, to the domain's DNS: only whoever
controls the domain can.

## Add it

Open **Domains**, in the side menu under **Trust**, and press **Add**.

<Screenshot name="registry-domain-new" alt="The screen to link a domain: the domain box and the Add button." />

Type the domain <Mark n={1} /> and press **Add** <Mark n={2} />.

## Prove it

<Screenshot name="registry-domains" alt="A domain not verified yet, opened to show its DNS record: type TXT, its name and value, and the Check button." />

<Steps>

1. Press the domain in the list to open it. Under **DNS record** <Mark n={2} />
   are the **Type** (TXT), the **Name** and the **Value**, each with a button
   to copy it.

2. In the place where your domain is managed (your provider's DNS panel, or
   whoever runs it for you), add a record with exactly that type, name and
   value.

3. Back here, press **Check** <Mark n={3} />. When the record is found, the
   domain shows **Verified**. If it is not found yet, wait a few minutes —
   DNS takes a while — and press **Check** again.

</Steps>

A verified domain goes into your organisation's identity, which then asks to
be signed again ([Pending](/use/organisation/pending/) says so). **Add**
<Mark n={1} /> links another domain; **Remove** unlinks one.

<Aside type="tip">
Leave the record in place: if it disappears, the next check takes the domain
out of your identity.
</Aside>
