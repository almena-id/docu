Your **Profile** is yours, not your organisation's: open it from the menu
under your initial, at the top right. There you set your **Alias** (the name
others see instead of your email), your **Ways in** — your email, your Almena
wallet, Google, Microsoft, Apple or GitHub — and your API tokens.

<Aside type="tip">
**Link Almena wallet** is how you get ready to sign for your organisation:
a code appears, and you scan it with your wallet.
</Aside>

## API tokens

An **API token** lets a program act as you: a script, a nightly job, the
`almena` command line. Whoever connects your systems will ask you for one.

<Steps>

1. Under **API tokens** <Mark n={1} />, type a **Name** <Mark n={3} /> that
   says where it will be used ("CI", "my laptop").

2. Choose the **Days valid** <Mark n={4} />, from 1 to 365.

3. Press **Create token** <Mark n={5} />. The token appears **shown this
   once**: press **Copy** and keep it where it will be used. The registry does
   not keep it.

</Steps>

Your tokens are listed <Mark n={2} /> with when they expire and when they
were last used. **Revoke** stops one at once.

<Aside type="caution">
A token acts as you in every account you belong to. Treat it like a key:
never share it in an email or a chat, and revoke it if you are unsure.
</Aside>
