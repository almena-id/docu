A **mediator** is a mailbox on the internet. Your phone is not always on, so
messages for you — from your contacts, from an organisation — wait there
until your wallet picks them up. It is also the address others reach you at.

A mediator cannot read what it keeps: every message is locked for you before
it leaves, and only your wallet opens it. It does not know what is said, nor
in a call what is heard.

## Which one

Almena runs a public mediator, **mediator.almena.id**, free for every wallet
and organisation: it is the one offered everywhere, and the right choice
unless someone you trust tells you otherwise — your organisation, for
example, if it runs its own. Any mediator that speaks the same standard
(DIDComm) works, as long as it uses a secure (HTTPS) address.

Whether Almena's mediator is working right now is on the [status
page](/use/status/).

## In your wallet

**Profile → Messaging**.

	<Screenshot phone name="wallet-mediator" alt="Connect to a mediator: the box with Almena's address already in it, and the Connect button." />
	<Screenshot phone from={3} name="wallet-messaging" alt="Messaging: connected to mediator.almena.id, with Check for messages and Disconnect." />
</Screens>

<Steps>

1. With none yet, the section says **Not connected**: press **Connect to a
   mediator**.

2. The box <Mark n={1} /> already has Almena's address. To use another one,
   paste its invitation link or its address instead.

3. Press **Connect** <Mark n={2} />.

</Steps>

Once connected, the section shows which one you use <Mark n={3} /> and how
many messages are waiting. **Check for messages** <Mark n={4} /> picks them
up now; **Disconnect** <Mark n={5} /> leaves it.

A mediator can also show you a code (its own page has one, as on
[mediator.almena.id](https://mediator.almena.id)): scan it with **Scan QR**.

<Screenshot phone name="wallet-mediator-invitation" alt="Connect to a mediator: the mediator offered, the one used now, and the Accept button." />

The wallet shows which mediator it is <Mark n={1} /> and the one you use now.
Press **Accept** <Mark n={2} /> to connect.

<Aside type="caution" title="Choose once">
Your contacts reach you through the mediator you had when you met them. If
you change it, their messages stop arriving. That is why, while you have
contacts through your current mediator, the wallet does not let a code or a
link change it. If a mediator says it is full, it is not taking new wallets
for now: try later, or choose another.
</Aside>

## For your organisation

In the registry, an organisation chooses a mediator in two places: for the
whole account, and for each issuer and verifier. People's wallets reach your
services through it.

<Screenshot name="registry-tenant" alt="The account's settings: name, mediator, the languages ticked and the Save button." />

<Steps>

1. Open **Settings** and choose the **Mediator** <Mark n={2} />: one of your
   own or a public one. A new account starts with Almena's; leave it unless
   you run your own. Press **Save**.

2. When you create an issuer or a verifier, its **Mediator** comes filled in
   the same way ([Set up an issuer](/use/organisation/issuers/)).

</Steps>

To run your own mediator, the account needs a [verified
domain](/use/organisation/domains/): a mediator answers on an address under
it, such as `mediator.nova.example`. Create it under **Mediators**, sign its
identity and publish it, like an issuer. A mediator you mark **public** is
offered to every organisation once published.
