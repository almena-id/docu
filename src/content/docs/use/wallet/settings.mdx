Everything you can change is on the wallet's last tab, **Profile**: your
picture and your name at the top, and below them one line per section.

<Screenshot phone name="wallet-profile" alt="The Profile tab: the picture and the name, and the list of sections: Profile, Appearance, Messaging, Backup, Security." />

- The square at the top right <Mark n={1} /> shows **your invitation**, the
  code someone scans to start talking with you ([Messages and
  calls](/use/wallet/messages/)).
- The sections <Mark n={2} />: **Profile**, **Appearance**, **Messaging**,
  **Backup** and **Security** — and, on a computer, **Notifications**. Press
  one to open it; the arrow at the top takes you back.

## Your name and picture

**Profile → Profile**.

<Screenshot phone name="wallet-profile-name" alt="The Profile section: the picture with its camera button, Change photo, and the Your name box with Save and send." />

<Steps>

1. Press the picture <Mark n={1} />, or **Change photo** <Mark n={2} />, to
   choose one. **Remove photo** appears once there is one.

2. Type the name your contacts will see <Mark n={3} /> and press **Save and
   send** <Mark n={4} />. Each contact gets it only through the address they
   know you by.

</Steps>

Organisations do not see this name: what they see is only what a credential
says, and only when you show it.

## Colour and mode

**Profile → Appearance**.

<Screenshot phone name="wallet-appearance" alt="Appearance: eight colours in a row, orange ticked, and the mode: System, Dark, Light." />

- **Identity colour** <Mark n={1} />: the colour the wallet wears — orange
  (the one it comes with), blue, red, yellow, green, cyan, magenta or teal.
  It changes at once.
- **Mode** <Mark n={2} />: **Light**, **Dark**, or **System** to follow your
  device.

<Aside type="note" title="The language">
The wallet speaks your device's language: there is no switch inside it.
Today it speaks English and Spanish; in any other language it speaks
English. To change it, change your phone's or computer's language.
</Aside>

## Notifications (on a computer)

**Profile → Notifications**, on Windows, Mac and Linux only: a phone is woken
by a notice that never says who wrote or what.

<Screenshot phone name="wallet-notifications" alt="Notifications: When a message arrives, with three choices — Everything, Who it is from, Nothing identifying." />

Under **When a message arrives** <Mark n={1} />, choose how much the
computer's notification says while the wallet is not in front:
**Everything** (who and what), **Who it is from**, or **Nothing
identifying**. The computer keeps what notifications show in its own history,
outside the wallet.

## Messaging and backup

**Profile → Messaging** is where your messages wait: see [Choose a
mediator](/use/mediator/). **Profile → Backup** saves and restores what your
recovery words do not bring back: see [Your wallet](/use/wallet/#a-copy-of-what-the-words-do-not-bring-back).

## Security

**Profile → Security**.

<Screenshot phone name="wallet-security" alt="Security: the PIN row with its digits, the Biometrics switch, Lock after 1, 5 or 10 minutes, where the identity is kept, and the Sign out button." />

- **PIN** <Mark n={1} />: how many digits it has. Press it to change it
  (below).
- **Biometrics** <Mark n={2} />: open the wallet with your face or your
  fingerprint as well as the PIN. Turning it on asks for the PIN once. When
  the device cannot do it, the line says **Not available on this device**.
- **Lock after** <Mark n={3} />: 1, 5 or 10 minutes without use, then the
  wallet locks itself.
- **Kept in**: where this device keeps your identity. Nothing to do here.
- **Sign out** <Mark n={4} />: see below.

On an iPhone set up without a PIN, the wallet opens with **Face ID or the
iPhone passcode** and has no PIN to change. On a computer there is also
**Open at login**: the wallet starts with the computer, minimised and locked.

## Change the PIN

<Flow
	label="Changing the PIN"
	steps={[
		{ icon: 'key', title: 'Your current PIN', text: 'So nobody who finds the wallet open can replace it.' },
		{ icon: 'wrench', title: 'Choose a new one', text: 'As many digits as the one you have.' },
		{ icon: 'check', title: 'Type it again', text: 'So a slip does not lock you out.' },
	]}
/>

<Screenshot phone name="wallet-pin-change" alt="Your current PIN: the empty dots and the keypad." />

Press **PIN** in **Security**. Type your current PIN on the keypad
<Mark n={2} />; the dots <Mark n={1} /> fill as you type. Then choose the new
one and type it again. Only the digits change: your identity, your
credentials and your conversations stay as they are.

<Aside type="caution" title="The attempts are counted">
After too many wrong PINs, the wallet removes the identity from this device,
and only your recovery words bring it back. When three or fewer attempts are
left, the lock screen says how many.
</Aside>

## Sign out

<Screenshot phone name="wallet-sign-out" alt="Sign out: what goes with it, the question Do you have your phrase in front of you?, the slider and Stay signed in." />

Signing out removes your identity from this device <Mark n={1} />, and your
conversations with it. Read the screen: there is no support line that can
undo it. The slider <Mark n={2} /> unlocks after a few seconds; slide it to
the end to sign out. **Stay signed in** <Mark n={3} /> takes you back.

Your recovery words are the only way back, on this device or any other.
Without them, the identity is gone for good. Make a [backup](/use/wallet/#a-copy-of-what-the-words-do-not-bring-back)
first if you want your credentials and conversations back too.
