You need the Almena wallet set up on your phone ([how](/use/wallet/)) and a
browser — on your computer, or on the phone itself.

## 1. Find the credential

Open [catalog.almena.id](https://catalog.almena.id). It lists every credential
you can ask for and who gives each one.

<Screenshot name="catalog-home" alt="The catalog's home: a search box, the categories and the credentials on offer, each as a card." />

<Steps>

1. Type what you are looking for in the search box <Mark n={1} />, for example
   "degree" or the name of the organisation.

2. Or narrow the list by category <Mark n={2} />: education, work, membership…

3. Open the credential you want <Mark n={3} />.

</Steps>

### Searching

The list changes as you type, a moment after the last key.

<Screenshot name="catalog-search" alt="The catalog searched for Academic: the two services found, the count and the button to clear the search." />

- The search box <Mark n={1} /> finds a word in the credential's name, the
  organisation's or the category's. The cross <Mark n={2} /> clears it, and
  **Clear filters** clears the search and the category together.
- What is found <Mark n={3} /> shows as cards, each with who gives it
  <Mark n={4} />. When nothing matches, the page says **No services match**:
  try other words, or another category.

### Who gives it

Press the name of the organisation on a card, or on a credential's page, to
see who it is and everything it gives.

<Screenshot name="catalog-issuer" alt="An issuer's page in the catalog: its initials and name, its description, its identity and the services it offers." />

- At the top, its name <Mark n={1} /> and what it does, and since when it is
  in the catalog.
- The long line under it is its identity on the internet, the same one its
  credentials carry. The button beside it <Mark n={2} /> copies it, for
  whoever wants to check it.
- **Services** <Mark n={3} />: every credential it gives. Open one <Mark n={4} />
  or press **Apply** under it.

## 2. Start the application

The credential's page says who gives it, what it says about you and what you
will be asked for.

<Screenshot name="catalog-offer" alt="A credential's page in the catalog, with the Start button." />

Press **Start** <Mark n={1} />.

## 3. Pair your wallet

The page shows the steps of your application and a code.

<Screenshot name="catalog-apply" alt="The first step of an application: a QR code to scan with the wallet." />

<Steps>

1. The bar at the top <Mark n={1} /> shows where you are: pair the wallet,
   fill in, sign and send, receive.

2. Open the wallet and scan the code <Mark n={2} />. If you are on the phone
   that has the wallet, press **Open in the wallet** <Mark n={3} /> instead.

3. The wallet shows who you are applying to <Mark n={4} />. Check the name
   and press **Start** <Mark n={5} />.

   <Screenshot phone from={4} name="wallet-pair" alt="The wallet asks to start an application to Nova Academic Office for an academic qualification." />

</Steps>

The page moves on by itself once your wallet answers.

## 4. Fill in and send

<Steps>

1. Fill in the form. If the organisation asks for a credential you already
   have, press **Present from my wallet**: those fields come filled in and
   verified, and you do not type them.

2. Press **Continue**. A second code appears: scan it. Your wallet shows
   everything you are sending <Mark n={1} /> — read it; if it is right, press
   **Sign and send** <Mark n={2} />.

   <Screenshot phone name="wallet-submit" alt="The wallet shows every answer of the application before signing and sending it." />

</Steps>

<Aside type="note" title="Keep this browser">
Your application lives in this browser: there is no account to sign in to.
An application not sent within a day is removed, so fill it in in one go.
</Aside>

### Come back to an application

Closed the page? **My applications**, at the top right of the catalog
<Mark n={1} />, lists the applications started in this browser and still open
in it.

<Screenshot name="catalog-applications" alt="My applications: an application to Nova Academic Office for an academic qualification, not sent yet, with the Open button." />

Each one <Mark n={2} /> says who you applied to, for what, and how it stands
(**Started**, **In progress**, **Sent**, **Accepted**, **Not accepted**, **Issued**). **Open** <Mark n={3} /> takes you
back to where you left it — for example, to take the credential once it is
given. Another browser, or another device, does not see them.

## 5. Receive it

The organisation reviews your application and accepts or rejects it; the
page tells you which. Once it gives you the credential, the page shows a last
code under **Take it into your wallet**: scan it with the same wallet you
applied with — only that one can take it.

<Screenshot phone name="wallet-receive" alt="The wallet offers to keep the credential from Nova Academic Office." />

The wallet shows the credential <Mark n={1} />: press **Keep it**
<Mark n={2} />. That's it: it is in your wallet, on **Home**. See [your
credentials](/use/wallet/credentials/).
