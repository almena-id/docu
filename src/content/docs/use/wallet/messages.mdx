The wallet is also a private messenger: you write to and call the people you
choose, and only the two of you can read or hear what is said. Nobody gets
your phone number or your email: a contact knows you by an address made for
the two of you alone.

You need a **mediator** first: the mailbox that holds your messages while
your device is off. The wallet offers Almena's; see [Choose a
mediator](/use/mediator/).

## Add a contact

### Show your invitation

On **Profile**, press the square at the top right.

<Screenshot phone name="wallet-invite" alt="Your invitation: a QR code, and the Copy the link button." />

Whoever scans this code <Mark n={1} /> with their Almena wallet can start a
conversation with you. If they are not in front of you, **Copy the link**
<Mark n={2} /> and send it to them any way you like. Each person who accepts
gets an address of their own, so nobody can tell who else you talk to.

### Accept someone's invitation

Scan their code with **Scan QR**, or open the link they sent you on the
device that has the wallet.

<Screenshot phone name="wallet-contact-invitation" alt="Contact invitation: Somebody wants to start a conversation with you, with the fingerprint and the Accept button." />

The wallet shows a **Contact invitation** <Mark n={1} />. The
**Fingerprint** is a short code of the other person's card: if you want to be
sure, ask them to read theirs aloud. Press **Accept** <Mark n={2} />, or
**Cancel**.

Until they answer, their line says **Waiting for them to answer**, and you
can write once they do.

## Your conversations

The **Messages** tab lists them, the most recent first.

<Screenshot phone name="wallet-messages" alt="Messages: Ana Torres with two unread, Marco Rossi, and a contact still waiting to answer." />

- Each line <Mark n={1} /> is a contact: the latest message, when, and how
  many you have not read.
- The round arrow <Mark n={2} /> checks for new messages now. They also arrive
  by themselves while the wallet is open.
- **+** <Mark n={3} /> opens **New conversation**.

To delete a conversation, swipe its line to the left (on a computer, right
click it) and press **Delete**. The contact stays; only what was said goes.

<Screenshot phone name="wallet-new-conversation" alt="New conversation: the list of contacts, each with since when." />

**New conversation** lists your **Contacts** <Mark n={1} />, each with since
when you know each other, or **Waiting for them to answer** <Mark n={2} />.
Press one to write.

## Write

<Screenshot phone name="wallet-conversation" alt="A conversation with Ana Torres: her name at the top with the call buttons, the messages, and the box to write." />

<Steps>

1. Type in the **Message** box <Mark n={4} /> and press **Send**. Your
   messages <Mark n={3} /> are on the right, in your colour.

2. A message that could not go says **Not sent. Try again**: press it to
   send it again.

3. Press the name at the top <Mark n={1} /> to see the contact.

</Steps>

## Call

In a conversation, the telephone <Mark n={2} /> makes a voice call and the
camera beside it a video call. The wallet asks for the microphone and the
camera the first time.

<Screenshot phone name="wallet-call" alt="An incoming video call from Ana Torres, with the Decline and Accept buttons." />

When someone calls you, the wallet shows who <Mark n={1} /> and whether it is
a voice or a video call. Press **Decline** <Mark n={2} /> or **Accept**
<Mark n={3} />. During the call you can mute the microphone, turn the camera
off and **Hang up**. If the wallet is locked, unlock it to answer.

The call goes through your mediator's relay, so the other person never
learns where your device is. If your mediator does not relay calls, the
wallet says **Your mediator does not relay calls, so none can be made.**

## A contact

<Screenshot phone name="wallet-contact" alt="A contact: their name, fingerprint and since when, and Your name for them with the Save button." />

- **Contact** <Mark n={1} />: the name they gave themselves, the
  **Fingerprint** and since when you know each other.
- **Your name for them** <Mark n={2} />: a name only you see, on this device,
  shown instead of theirs. **Save** <Mark n={3} />; **Use their name** takes it
  away.

<Aside type="caution" title="Your conversations live on your device">
Messages are kept only on this device, encrypted for your identity. Signing
out deletes them, and your recovery words do not bring them back: a
[backup](/use/wallet/#a-copy-of-what-the-words-do-not-bring-back) does.
</Aside>
