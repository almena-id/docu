Your credentials are on the wallet's first tab, **Home**, each as a card.
Press one to see everything about it.

- **About it**: who issued it, when, until when it holds, and through which
  registry it arrived.
- **Status** <Mark n={1} />: how it stands, as its issuer last said.
  **Check status** <Mark n={2} /> asks again now.
- **What it says** <Mark n={3} />: every piece of data in it. When you show the
  credential, only the pieces that are asked for go — never all of them
  unless all are asked.
- **Remove from this wallet** <Mark n={4} />: takes it out for good, after
  asking you again.

## How a credential stands

| On the card | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Valid** | It holds: you can show it. |
| **Suspended** | The issuer has paused it, for now. The wallet does not offer it, and whoever checks it refuses it. It may be reinstated. |
| **Revoked** | The issuer has withdrawn it for good. Only the issuer can give you a new one. |

The wallet checks how each one stands when you open it, at most every ten
minutes, and whenever you press the round arrow on **Home**.

<Aside type="caution">
Removing a credential cannot be undone: nothing keeps a copy, and only its
issuer can give it to you again. A backup made before (**Profile → Backup**)
still has it.
</Aside>
