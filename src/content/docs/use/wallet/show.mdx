At a door, on a website, at an office: whoever asks to see a credential
shows you a code on their screen.

<Flow
	label="Showing a credential"
	steps={[
		{ icon: 'qr', title: 'They show a code', text: 'On their screen, or as a link on your phone.' },
		{ icon: 'scan', title: 'You scan it', text: 'From the Scan QR tab of the wallet.' },
		{ icon: 'shield-check', title: 'You decide', text: 'The wallet says who asks and exactly what would go.' },
		{ icon: 'badge-check', title: 'They check it', text: 'In a second: genuine, from its issuer, still valid.' },
	]}
/>

<Steps>

1. Open the wallet and the **Scan QR** tab, and point the camera at the code.

2. The wallet shows who is asking and what for <Mark n={1} />: the
   credential that would go and, in it, only the data they asked for.

   <Screenshot phone name="wallet-verify" alt="The wallet asks whether to present a membership credential to Campus Access, listing the two pieces of data that would go." />

3. If that is who you expect, press **Present** <Mark n={2} />. If not,
   **Cancel**: nothing is sent.

</Steps>

Whoever asked sees on their screen that the credential is genuine, who issued
it and that it still holds — and only the data they asked for.

Are you the one asking? [Check credentials](/use/organisation/verifiers/)
explains how an organisation shows the code and reads the answer.

<Aside type="tip" title="Only accept what you started">
Accept only when you are in front of whoever the wallet names and you expect
them to ask. A code you did not expect can come from anyone.
</Aside>
