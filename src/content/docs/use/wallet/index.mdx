The Almena wallet is an app for your phone or your computer. It keeps your
credentials and shows them, only when you say so. It works on iPhone and
Android, and on Windows, Mac and Linux computers.

## Set it up

<Screens>
	<Screenshot phone name="wallet-welcome" alt="The wallet's welcome: create an identity, or bring one back with a phrase." />
	<Screenshot phone from={3} name="wallet-phrase" alt="The twelve words of the recovery phrase, numbered, with the choice of 12 or 24 words." />
</Screens>

<Steps>

1. Open the wallet and press **Create an identity** <Mark n={1} />. If you
   already had one and wrote down its words, press **I already have a
   phrase** <Mark n={2} /> instead.

2. The wallet shows your **recovery words** <Mark n={4} />: twelve words,
   numbered. Twelve is enough; you can choose twenty-four at the top
   <Mark n={3} />.

3. Write them down on paper, in order. Tick that you have them and press
   **Continue**.

4. The wallet asks you for some of the words, to check you wrote them right.

5. Choose a **PIN**. Where your phone allows it, you can also open the wallet
   with your face or your fingerprint.

</Steps>

<Aside type="caution" title="Your recovery words are your wallet">
Nobody can give them back to you: not Almena, not anyone. Whoever has them
has your wallet. Never type them into a website or send them to anyone —
Almena will never ask for them.
</Aside>

## Every time you open it

<Screens>
	<Screenshot phone name="wallet-lock" alt="The locked wallet asks for the PIN on a keypad." />
	<Screenshot phone from={3} name="wallet-home" alt="The wallet's home: each credential as a card, with the tabs below." />
</Screens>

The wallet opens locked: type your PIN on the keypad <Mark n={2} />; the dots
<Mark n={1} /> fill as you type. It locks itself again after a few minutes
without use.

Then you see **Your wallet**: each credential you hold, as a card
<Mark n={3} /> with how it stands — **Valid**, **Suspended**, **Revoked**. The
round arrow <Mark n={4} /> checks them all again. **Scan QR** <Mark n={5} />
opens the camera, for the codes the catalog and others show you.

## A copy of what the words do not bring back

<Screenshot phone name="wallet-backup" alt="Profile, Backup: save a backup, or restore one." />

The recovery words bring back your identity, but not what you received with
it: your credentials, your contacts and your conversations. In **Profile →
Backup**, **Save a backup** <Mark n={1} /> saves all of that in one locked
file; only your words open it. Keep that file somewhere other than your
phone. On a new device, after typing your words, **Choose a backup**
<Mark n={2} /> brings it all back.
