---
title: Glossary
description: The words of Almena, in plain language.
---

**Account**
: An organisation in the registry, with the people who work in it. One
  person can be in several accounts.

**Catalog**
: The website where people find the credentials they can ask for:
  [catalog.almena.id](https://catalog.almena.id).

**Credential**
: A digital card or certificate that says something about you — a degree, a
  membership, a job — signed by whoever gave it.

**Holder**
: The person a credential is about, who keeps it in their wallet.

**Identity (DID)**
: The public name of an organisation or of one of its services, written so
  that anyone can find its keys and check its signatures. Almena publishes it.

**Issuer**
: The part of an organisation that gives credentials, such as a university's
  academic office.

**Mediator**
: A mailbox on the internet that keeps messages for a wallet while it is off,
  without being able to read them.

**QR code**
: The square of dots you scan with your wallet. It carries the request, never
  your data.

**Recovery words**
: The words your wallet shows you when you set it up. With them, and only
  with them, you get your wallet back on a new device. Keep them safe and
  never share them.

**Registry**
: The website where organisations set themselves up, receive applications
  and give credentials: [registry.almena.id](https://registry.almena.id).

**Revoke / suspend**
: Withdraw a credential for good / for a while. Anyone checking it sees it.

**Signature**
: The digital stamp of whoever gives a credential, or of the wallet that
  sends something. If anything changes, the signature no longer matches.

**Trust anchor**
: Almena itself: the account that vouches for the platform and keeps the
  catalogue of credential types.

**Verifier**
: Whoever asks you to show a credential, and checks it.

**Wallet**
: The Almena app where you keep your credentials and show them, on your phone
  or computer.
