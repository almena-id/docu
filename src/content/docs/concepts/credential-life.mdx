## Find it and ask for it

The [catalog](https://catalog.almena.id) lists every credential you can ask
for. You choose one, and the page shows a code to scan with your wallet: this
links your wallet to your request, so the credential will be made for it and
nobody else. Then you fill in the form and send it, signed by your wallet.

[Step by step, with pictures →](/use/wallet/apply/)

## It is checked

The organisation sees your request in its registry: your answers, and the
proof that your wallet sent them. It accepts it or rejects it, and may leave
you a note.

## Receive it

Once accepted, the organisation signs your credential. The request page in
the catalog shows a last code: scan it and the credential is in your wallet.

## Show it

When someone asks for a credential — at a door, on a website, at an
office — they show you a code. You scan it, your wallet tells you who is
asking and exactly what they would see, and you decide. They see only what
was asked for: if they need your name, they do not see your date of birth.

## Suspended or withdrawn

A credential can stop holding: a membership ends, a card is lost. The
organisation can **suspend** it (for a while) or **revoke** it (for good).
Your wallet shows how each of your credentials stands, and anyone checking
one sees it too.
